# The written bound $\mu(G)\le528.23288$

For every integer $p$ and every sufficiently large positive integer $q$,

$$
|G-p/q|>q^{-528.23288}.
$$

This uses the [arbitrary-degree inverse-image quadrature construction](inverse_image_general_degree.md),
the [trimmed support and eventual near-field argument](trimmed_support.md),
and the inherited determinant, arithmetic, and nonvanishing theorems
identified in [the first derivation](measure.md). The new finite inequalities
are checked with exact rational arithmetic. This page records the written
proof; it does not assert completion of the separate Lean formalization.

## Eventual energy estimate

The exact first trial coefficient is

$$
u_1=\frac{199568949785990693712071}{1099511627776000000000000}.
$$

For $N\ge10^6$, the coefficient $(48N-1)/(58N)$ is at least
$\alpha=47999999/58000000$. A rational cubic root enclosure and logarithm
bounds give a strict upper bound below $-0.78145547$ for

$$
F(y)=-\frac{53}{58}\log(1+y^2)+\alpha\log y-u_1y,
\qquad 0<y<1.
$$

The grouped field has the additional term $\log(1+y^2)/(58N)$.
The same certificate verifies that adding $\log2/(58\cdot10^7)$ still
leaves the bound below $-0.78145547$. Thus the full grouped near field has
cap $\mathcal N_c=-0.78145547$ for every $N\ge10^7$.

This is an eventual version of the inherited near-field hypothesis. The
upstream record asking for every positive $N$ cannot be reused verbatim.
The written proof applies the pointwise second-case estimate after
intersecting its tail with $N\ge10^7$, as detailed in the support argument.
All later conclusions are already eventual in $N$.
