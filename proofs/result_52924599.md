# The written bound $\mu(G)\le529.24599$

For every integer $p$ and every sufficiently large positive integer $q$,

$$
|G-p/q|>q^{-529.24599}.
$$

This strengthens the [degree-53 result](result_5306705.md) by improving the
auxiliary polynomial used to choose interpolation nodes. It uses the
[arbitrary-degree quadrature argument](inverse_image_general_degree.md),
the same inherited determinant, arithmetic, nonvanishing, and energy theorems,
and the exact finite certificates below. It is a written proof,
not a new Lean formalization.

## Node and circle certificates

The energy support is unchanged: $[-1,-0.1573]\cup[0.0857,1]$.
The rational polynomial in `certificates/inverse_image_107.json` has degree
107. Alternating control values of absolute value greater than one give
51 inverse branches in the left band and 56 in the right band, for every
level in $[-1,1]$. All roots are simple. Use

$$
K_N=\left\lceil\frac{53N}{107}\right\rceil.
$$

The roots of $T_{K_N}(P)$ supply at least $53N$ nodes. Removing at most 106
surplus roots leaves exactly $53N$ nodes. The removal and ceiling change
only a constant prefactor, as proved in the arbitrary-degree argument.

At radius $r=1+10^{-8}$, exact autocorrelation and 34 Bernstein pieces prove

$$
(2|P(z)|+1)^{53}|1-z|^{642}\le e^{107\cdot51.2974}
\quad (|z|=r).
$$

Here $642=53\cdot12+6$. The checker uses $Q(z)=P(z)(1-z)^{12}$ and retains
the remaining factor $|1-z|^6$ separately on each cell. This is the filtered
circle certificate in the general proof. Take $w=51.297401>51.2974$.

The SHA-256 of the exact polynomial witness is
`396f8de2f67df1729a7b7aaa9ff9c451d866e38cf981569b61a77587f1d7bfb7`.

$$
0<\eta<3364U-\frac{16129}{8}\log2-\frac{24733}{4}+\chi,
