# The written bound mu(G) <= 530.6705

Let $G$ be Catalan's constant. For every integer $p$ and every sufficiently large
positive integer $q$,

$$
|G-p/q|>q^{-530.6705}.
$$

This follows from the [polynomial inverse-image quadrature argument](inverse_image_quadrature.md),
the inherited determinant and arithmetic theorems identified in
[the first derivation](measure.md), and the exact certificates below.
It is a written proof with rational certificates, not a new Lean formalization.

## Certified data

The unchanged arithmetic gain is

$$

$$

The new rational degree-53 polynomial has 25 inverse branches on the negative

With $\ell=\log2$, the unchanged norm, field, residual, and near-field caps are
$\mathcal N=0.7736205195$, $\Phi=-2.53949899$, $\mathcal R=-0.0631062$,
and $\mathcal N_c=-0.77$.
The scalar certificate checks, using outward rational bounds for $\ell$,

$$
m=\frac{41}{58}\ell-\mathcal N-\Phi-U>0,
\qquad
\left(\frac{76}{29}\ell-\Phi+\mathcal R-c_2\right)^2<8\ell m,
$$

$$
\ell-\mathcal R+\mathcal N_c<0,
\qquad
0<\eta<3364U-\frac{16129}{8}\ell-\frac{24733}{4}+\chi.
$$

The first energy case still holds because $U$ is smaller than the inherited
target and its fixed-column cost $4.09661$ is smaller than $c_2$.
Thus the integer polynomials are at most $e^{-\eta N^2}$ on
$|x-G|\le e^{-\rho N}$ for all sufficiently large $N$.

For $\kappa=B/\rho$, all numerical conditions of the unchanged prime-selection
criterion hold. In particular,

$$
\frac{53\rho}{\eta-212\delta}
=\frac{39858271805250}{75109266697}
<530.6705.
$$

This proves the stated rational-approximation inequality and hence
$\mu(G)\le530.6705$. The strict decay inequality leaves room for the
asymptotic error terms. No explicit threshold for $q$ is asserted.
